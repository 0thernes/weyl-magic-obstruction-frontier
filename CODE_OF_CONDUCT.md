# Code of Conduct (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`)

All participants in this project commit to fostering an open, rigorous, respectful, and harassment-free scientific and engineering community, regardless of background, identity, or affiliation.

## Expected Behavior
- Ground technical and mathematical critiques in verifiable proofs, counterexamples, reproducible commands, and primary references.
- Welcome independent audits, skepticism, and constructive errata reports.
- Treat all collaborators and reviewers with courtesy and intellectual honesty.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Personal attacks, harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, or publication of private personal information.
- Fabrication of citations, benchmarks, proof certificates, or verification outputs.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns privately to Alexander Stephen Donahue at `0_0@0thernes.art`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
