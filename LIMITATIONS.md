# LIMITATIONS.md — Scope, Epistemic Boundaries, and Real vs. Metaphor

This document states explicitly what `weyl-magic-obstruction-frontier` (`0TH-2026-022`) **does not prove**, where its formal guarantees stop, and how classical software cross-checks are distinguished from physical quantum claims.

## 1. Mathematical & Formal Scope Boundaries

1. **Non-peer-reviewed preprint:** All theorems in `lean/` are machine-checked by Lean 4.35.0 and five additional external kernels, which guarantees logical derivation from standard Lean axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`) — **not** peer review, mathematical significance, or immunity to specification mismatch. Readers should audit `lean/PalomarChallenge.lean`, `lean/WeylMagic/Basic.lean`, and [docs/statement-fidelity.md](docs/statement-fidelity.md) directly to verify statement fidelity.
2. **$n=4$ Equimodularity / Lagrangian Hypothesis (`T2e`):** Theorem `T2e` (and its arbitrary-bent Lean bridge `n4_z4_bent_hyperplane_rupture_arbitrary_bent`) rules out **equimodular / Lagrangian** $n=4$ qubit WH-MUB states (states with equal computational-basis magnitudes $|c_x| = 1/4$). While our deterministic C++20 Riemannian L-BFGS search (`64` starts, reaching $\Phi_4 \approx 2.150520154714$) finds no non-equimodular state below the two-stabilizer stationary point $|\Psi_4(\theta^*)\rangle$, **global optimality of $|\Psi_4(\theta^*)\rangle$ over all of $\mathbb{C}^{16}$ is NOT formally proved** (`[OPEN]`).
3. **Even $n \ge 6$ Qubits:** Pillar 1 (`T1c`) is unconditional for **all odd $n \ge 3$**. The even-$n$ quaternary bent-hyperplane analysis in Pillar 2 is proved universally up to Rothaus's bound (`T2c`, all $m \ge 2$) and completed through the exhaustive 3-hyperplane zero-sum contradiction specifically at $n = 4$ (`T2d`, `T2e`, `n4_z4_bent_hyperplane_rupture_arbitrary_bent`). The strong form for even $n \ge 6$ remains `[OPEN]`.
4. **Characteristic-3 Alltop Prior Work & Scope:** Existence of complete Weyl–Heisenberg MUBs in characteristic $3$ via Galois rings $\mathrm{GR}(9, n)$ is established by Damski et al. (`arXiv:2603.15550`). Our Pillar 5 contribution (`T5a`–`T5c`) is an elementary $\mathbb{Z}_9$ divided-power carry formulation and machine-checked Lean 4.35.0 $\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_9]$ certificates for $n = 1, 2, 3$ (plus exact Rust/C11 verification at $n = 4$, `D = 81`). Lean kernel verification for $n \ge 4$ is not claimed inside Lean (it is `[EXACT-COMP]`, not `[LEAN-6K]`).
5. **Pillar 3 $\alpha = 4$ Re-Optimization & Non-Cyclic WH-SICs:** While `v42` and `v44` are machine-checked (`[LEAN-6K]`, 0 axioms) to beat WH-MUB at $\alpha = 2, 3$ and reverse at $\alpha = 4$, the further finding that direct L-BFGS re-optimization at $\alpha = 4$ still beats WH-MUB (`1.012971 < 1.013672` and `1.003658 < 1.003662`) is strictly `[NUMERICAL-APPROX]` until exact algebraic $\alpha = 4$ witnesses are constructed. Nonexistence of non-cyclic WH-SICs in $W(4)\otimes W(2)$ and $W(4)\otimes W(4)$ also remains `[OPEN]`.
6. **Zero-Mathlib Trust Base Tradeoff:** Avoiding Mathlib keeps the trust base tiny (cold build in seconds, zero external Lean dependencies) and eliminates dependency drift, but means algebraic structures (finite fields, cyclotomic rings, Fubini–Study fractions) are encoded directly in `lean/WeylMagic/Basic.lean` rather than reusing Mathlib's `ZMod`/`CyclotomicField` APIs.

## 2. Real vs. Metaphor (Classical Cross-Checks vs. Quantum Hardware)

| Component | What It Actually Is (Real) | What It Is NOT (Explicit Disclaimer) |
|---|---|---|
| **QGTL (`third_party/qgtl`, `c/src/qgtl_bridge.c`)** | Classical C11 finite-difference Richardson extrapolation of the Fubini–Study metric tensor on CPU (`diffgeo_distance` only). | Not a quantum computer, not a formal proof, not a physical quantum experiment. |
| **Moonlab (`third_party/moonlab`, `node/moonlab_crosscheck.cjs`)** | Classical WebAssembly CPU state-vector simulator cross-checking double-precision expectations to $1.33 \times 10^{-14}$. | Not a physical quantum processor or hardware noise benchmark. |
| **QRNG Stream (`c/src/qrng_stream.c`)** | Deterministic classical 64-bit counter-mode integer mixer (`splitmix64` + `hadamard_mix` + `absorb_seed`, verbatim MIT block from `tsotchke/quantum_rng` commit `1a77e77f`), bit-exact across C11, Node BigInt, and Python, used solely to seed L-BFGS and fuzzers reproducibly. | Not physical quantum random number generation and not a cryptographic entropy source; explicitly deterministic for reproducibility. |
| **Eshkol (`eshkol/magic_qgt_ad.esk`)** | Source-level Eshkol automatic-differentiation companion specification mirroring `T4b` and `T5d`. **Not compiled or executed in CI** (no `eshkol` compiler binary is installed on the verification runner). | Not an executed CI check and not a deductive proof; formal proofs live in Lean `T4b`/`T5d` and executable QGT checks run in C/QGTL and Python. |
| **WebGL2 Observatory (`docs/site/index.html`)** | Client-side 3D WebGL2 (with 2D canvas fallback) visualization evaluating the exact closed-form tower formulas and exact `v42`/`v44` integer witnesses locally in the browser. | Not a live compute backend or scientific oracle. |
