# Support

## Getting Help

- **Reproducing results or running the verifiers:** Start with [README.md](README.md#quickstart), [REPLICATION.md](REPLICATION.md), and [docs/faq.md](docs/faq.md).
- **Reporting a bug, proof gap, or replication discrepancy:** Open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/0thernes/weyl-magic-obstruction-frontier/issues) using the structured issue templates in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`.
- **Academic correspondence, statement-fidelity questions, or arXiv endorsement inquiries:** Contact Alexander Stephen Donahue at `0_0@0thernes.art` (ORCID [`0009-0006-8168-9648`](https://orcid.org/0009-0006-8168-9648)).
- **Security or proof-exploit disclosures:** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Maintenance Expectations

This is an independent open-research repository maintained on a best-effort basis. Reproducible bug reports, Lean kernel failures, and mathematical errata are prioritized.
